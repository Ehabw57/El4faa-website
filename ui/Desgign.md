## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a dignified, trustworthy, and community-rooted civic presence for the Al-Shifa Medical Complex and Al-Madina Mosque initiative in Al-Ulayqat (Qus, Qena), governed by Jam'iyat Abnaa Oqail. 

### Brand Personality & Core Mood
- **Dignified & Serene:** Grounded in sacred purpose and humanitarian service. Avoids sensationalism, loud fundraising banners, and commercial hype.
- **Trustworthy & Accountable:** Prioritizes structural clarity, meticulous transparency, and legible donation metrics to cultivate long-term donor confidence and communal ownership.
- **Human-Centered & Warm:** Conveys genuine pastoral care and public health solidarity for Upper Egypt's rural community.

### Design Movement: Modern Civic Minimalism with Tactile Restraint
The aesthetic relies on strict editorial structure, spacious composition, crisp hairline dividers, and deliberate typographic rhythm. Glassmorphic distortions, harsh neon accents, and flashy hover animations are strictly excluded in favor of calm contrast, respectful white space, and understated materiality.

## Colors

The palette establishes an immediate balance between medical service (restorative forest greens) and sacred endowment (understated antique gold).

### Palette Hierarchy & Intent
- **Primary Green (`#1B7A52` / `#145E3E`):** Represents health, communal renewal, and charitable care. Used for primary interactive actions, high-level structural markers, and medical service indicators.
- **Accent Gold (`#C8963E` / `#B8822B`):** Denotes the spiritual sanctuary of the mosque, communal generosity, and selective milestones. Kept intentionally matte and low-gloss.
- **Surfaces & Canvas:** Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) forms the primary base, accompanied by quiet, warm neutral gray tiers (`#F8F9FA`, `#F1F3F5`, `#E9ECEF`) to distinguish content blocks and donation breakdown cards without heavy borders.
- **Typography & Details:** High-legibility deep charcoal (`#1A202C`, `#2D3748`) ensures crisp, fatigue-free reading in bright outdoor environments, supported by slate gray (`#64748B`) for metadata, administrative notes, and timestamps.
- **Hairlines & Dividers:** Subtle soft neutral (`#E2E8F0`) provides structural containment without visual clutter.

### Semantic Badge System
- **مكتمل (Completed):** Soft sage tint (`#E8F5E9`), deep emerald text (`#1B5E20`).
- **جارٍ توفيره (In Progress):** Warm sand tint (`#FEF3C7`), ochre amber text (`#92400E`).
- **مطلوب (Required):** Neutral slate wash (`#F1F5F9`), slate text (`#334155`).
- **عاجل (Urgent):** Subdued crimson tint (`#FEF2F2`), deep garnet text (`#991B1B`). Flashing effects are strictly avoided.

## Typography

Typography is calibrated for native right-to-left (RTL) Arabic reading conventions using **IBM Plex Sans Arabic**. Its rational, humanist grotesque stroke matches civic seriousness while maintaining exceptional legibility across dense medical manifests and funding sheets.

### Typographic Rules
- **Line Heights:** Arabic text requires expansive vertical clearance compared to Latin scripts. Line heights are calibrated between 1.55x and 1.75x to prevent diacritic clipping and word collision.
- **Numbers & Accounting:** Arabic numerals (١، ٢، ٣) and standard figures (1, 2, 3) must use proportional figures for financial reports and tabular figures for equipment ledger rows.
- **Weight Pairing:** Headings use Medium (500) and Bold (700) sparingly. Primary body paragraphs use Regular (400) to preserve airy readability.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined 12-column responsive fluid grid anchored by comfortable vertical white space.

### Structural Rhythms
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid, 32px (`margin`) outer boundaries, 20px (`gutter`) column spacing. Content max-width is constrained to 1200px to maintain reading focus.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8-column grid, 24px outer margins, 16px gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, 16px (`margin-mobile`) boundaries, 12px (`gutter-mobile`) gutters. Stacked cards and forms prioritize single-column clarity to support non-technical and elder community users.

### RTL Grid Flow
All column progressions, flex flows, and visual hierarchies start strictly from the top-right and traverse toward the left. Spacers never cause horizontal scrolling on smaller screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses tonal surface shifting and calm low-contrast outlines rather than drop shadows.

### Surface Tiers
- **Base Canvas:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Recessed Surface:** `#F8F9FA` for overall canvas grounding, page backdrops, and table header rows.
- **Card Surface:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) layered directly over `#F8F9FA` or `#F1F3F5`, enclosed by a single `#E2E8F0` hairline border.

### Shadow Guidance
When elevated elements (dialogs, sticky mobile contribution footers) require distinction:
- **Quiet Lift:** `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(26, 32, 44, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(26, 32, 44, 0.03)`
- **Modal Elevation:** `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(26, 32, 44, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(26, 32, 44, 0.04)`
No saturated green or gold colored drop shadows are permitted.

## Shapes

The design uses **Soft** geometry (`roundedness: 1`), conveying civic structure, architectural stability, and medical order.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Badges):** `4px` (`0.25rem`) corner radius.
- **Cards, Panels, and Callout Boxes:** `8px` (`0.5rem`) corner radius.
- **Modals and Major Section Wrappers:** `12px` (`0.75rem`) corner radius.
- **Pills:** Permitted exclusively for concise status tags and category pills (`9999px`).

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Contribution / Main Action):** Solid `#1B7A52` background, white label, `4px` radius. Hover shifts smoothly to `#145E3E`. Active state compresses slightly without bounce.
- **Secondary (Mosque / Endowment Specific):** Solid `#C8963E` background, white label, hover state `#B8822B`.
- **Tertiary / Outline:** `#FFFFFF` background, hairline border in `#E2E8F0`, deep slate label (`#1A202C`). On hover, surface shifts to `#F8F9FA`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Text inputs, donation amount selectors, and dropdowns share a clean `#FFFFFF` base, `1px` border in `#CBD5E1`, and `4px` radius.
- Focus state: Replaces border with `#1B7A52` and adds a subtle, non-intrusive focus ring (`rgba(27, 122, 82, 0.15)`).
- Prefix and suffix markers (e.g., "ج.م" or Egyptian Pound indicators) sit anchored to the opposite reading direction inside the input.

### Project & Donation Cards
- Constructed on a pure white surface with a crisp `#E2E8F0` border and `8px` corner radius.
- Top segment holds categorical labels (`قسم العيادات`, `فرش المسجد`, `محطة المياه`) paired with semantic status badges.
- Progress bars use an unfilled neutral channel (`#E9ECEF`) with a solid fill in `#1B7A52` (or `#C8963E` for mosque-specific targets).
- Financial metrics are displayed in high-contrast charcoal text with secondary labels in slate gray.

### Status Badges
- Compact padding (`0.25rem 0.625rem`), font size `12px` (`label-md`), `4px` radius.
- Strictly flat background tints (10–15% opacity of the semantic color) with high-contrast text. No decorative icon clutter.

### Transparency Ledger / Manifest Lists
- Tabular itemization of village medical supplies and construction expenditures.
- Alternating row styling using `#FFFFFF` and `#F8F9FA`.
- Header row styled in `#F1F3F5` with bold, concise column labels and fine horizontal borders.
